Who I Am

About Me

AI and data-science researcher specializing in remote sensing, computer vision, multimodal fusion, and intelligent sensing for real-world decision support.

I am an AI and data-science researcher specializing in remote sensing, computer vision, multimodal data fusion, spatiotemporal modeling, and intelligent sensing. I build computational methods that connect machine learning with observations from satellites, UAVs, IoT systems, environmental sensors, and field experiments.

At the University of Florida UF/IFAS TREC, I develop scalable AI workflows for agricultural and environmental applications including drone-image stitching and mapping, field-boundary extraction, water-stress analysis, disease identification, soil-moisture forecasting, sensor calibration, and multi-horizon prediction from weather and high-frequency field data.

Previously I served as Assistant Professor of Computer Science and Director of International Partnerships at Debre Markos University, and completed postdoctoral and doctoral research in Taiwan on localization, mobility prediction, UAV-assisted communication, and object tracking. My goal is accurate, scalable, and robust AI that turns heterogeneous data into useful decisions for agriculture, climate resilience, public safety, and healthcare.
